# EveryGen AI

**Create AI images, videos, and more with one creative toolkit**

🌐 Website: [EveryGen AI](https://everygenai.app/)

## About

EveryGen AI brings AI image generation, AI video generation and handy creative utilities into one web app. Describe what you want or upload a photo to create images and short videos, use task-focused pages for common jobs, prepare pictures with in-browser tools, and rewrite copy for different audiences and tones. No installs, works in the browser.

## Get started

Visit [https://everygenai.app/](https://everygenai.app/) to try EveryGen AI in your browser — no install required.
